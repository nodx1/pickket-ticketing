Pickket — Your daily companion for live music and shows

Not just another ticket app. Pickket turns ticket buying into an experience: swipe through a personalized Discover feed, chat with an AI concierge, book together with friends, count down to show day, and keep every ticket in a digital wallet — all in one place.

■ Key Features

• Discover Feed
A TikTok-style full-screen vertical pager. Double-tap to favorite, long-press to share, swipe to dive into details.

• AI Concierge
"Find me a jazz show in Gangnam this weekend" — ask in plain language, by voice or text. The more you use it, the better it learns your taste.

• Group Booking
Hold 4 seats, share an invite link, and let friends pay their own tickets.

• D-Day Countdown
From booking to show day: daily countdowns, setlist previews, venue routes, and entry guides.

• Digital Wallet
Tickets, memberships, and points in one hub. Apple Wallet and Google Pay pass support.

• Active Notifications
Location-based alerts, price drop watch, weekly curation, friend activity — all opt-in. Choose exactly what reaches you.

• Biometric Payment
Face ID / fingerprint authentication at checkout. Tokens stored securely in SecureStore.

• Store-ready
Apple Sign-In, Privacy Manifest, explicit consent screens, crash reporting, update enforcement — all compliance work completed.

■ Why Pickket

- Fair ticketing — queue system, idempotency keys, rotating TOTP QR codes to block illegal resale
- Automatic refunds — instant reversal on failed or cancelled payments
- Privacy-first — every active notification is opt-in
- Accessible — 44pt touch targets, VoiceOver/TalkBack support
